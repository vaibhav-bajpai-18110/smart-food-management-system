About Our Mission

Every day, tons of edible food is wasted while many people sleep hungry. Our platform helps reduce food waste by enabling quick and safe food donation.

🍱

Surplus Food Collection

Restaurants and events can list extra food with quantity, location, and pickup time.

🤝

NGO Connection

🚚

Smart Delivery

Volunteers can pick up food and deliver it to needy communities quickly.

How It Works

A simple process to rescue food and serve communities.

1️⃣

Post Donation

Donor uploads food details, location, and available pickup time.

2️⃣

Match NGO

The system finds nearby NGOs or volunteers based on food quantity and distance.

3️⃣

Deliver Food

Food is collected and distributed to people in need before expiry.
